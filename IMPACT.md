# Why this matters to an organization

## Who feels the pain

Support teams drown in repetitive tickets (passwords, stock, status checks)
while new customers wait on manual onboarding. A plain chatbot answers
confidently and acts never; hiring for 24/7 coverage is expensive.

## What changes with this system

- **Agents that act, with receipts.** KB lookup, inventory checks, ticket
  creation and welcome emails happen inside one supervised run — and the
  trajectory log shows every step for audit.
- **Escalation by design, not by accident.** Legal threats, anger and
  explicit human requests route to a specialist with a recorded reason,
  instead of getting a fluent brush-off.
- **Trajectory evals as a trust story.** Golden tasks assert the agent took
  the right tools in the right order — the thing leadership needs before
  letting automation touch customers.

## Metrics a team would track

- Cost per resolved ticket; median time-to-resolution; after-hours coverage.
- Escalation rate + reasons (tuning signal, not failure).
- Trajectory-eval pass rate on the golden set across model/prompt changes.
